CHAINSHIFT

Chainshift: Orb Puzzle for iPhone. Tap. Shift. Chain.

Contact support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Email egemen.sag@icloud.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

How to play

Tap any orb. It explodes together with every connected orb of the same color. Each orb next to the blast shifts to the next color in the cycle: blue, pink, yellow, violet.

Bigger groups score much more. Clear 6 or more orbs for a bonus move, 12 or more for two. Each run has 25 moves.

Frequently asked

Is my progress saved? Your best scores are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Does the game need an internet connection? No. It works fully offline and needs no account.

Is the Daily Challenge the same for everyone? Yes. Every player gets the same board on each calendar day (UTC).
