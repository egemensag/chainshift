Privacy Policy

Chainshift: Orb Puzzle · Effective October 23, 2026

Summary

Chainshift does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The game works entirely on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game records (such as best scores) locally on your iPhone using standard iOS storage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Third-party services

The app contains no third-party SDKs, analytics tools or advertising networks.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a future version adds an optional feature, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
